HOSPITALITY 2035
Project Overview — Landing Page, E-Commerce & Event Registration
Purpose: This document summarizes what we are building, the agreed functional scope, the supplied content
structure, and the technical requirements for implementation. It is intended as a working brief for design and
development.
Project objective
Build a premium, editorial-style landing page for the study Hospitality 2035 – How the economic logic of the
hotel industry is changing. The website will present the study, support direct digital sales, provide information
and registration for Berlin and Zurich events, present study/industry partners, and provide enquiry paths for team
and company licenses.
Core content supplied by the client
• Study title, positioning and introductory copy.
• Study preview and cover presentation structure.
• Key theses and strategic themes around costs, staffing, AI, digitization, robotics, automation, business models and
hotel operations.
• Target audiences: hotel operators/groups; owners, investors and project developers; technology/solutions
providers; banks, financiers and consultants; associations and industry organizations.
• Berlin and Zurich event content structure.
• Current license/pricing content and enquiry forms.
• Study partner / industry partner structure and DICON company information.
1. Landing page structure
The page should be designed as a long-form, high-quality editorial landing page with strong visual hierarchy and
clear conversion paths.
Hero / Above the fold
• DICON branding.
• Hospitality 2035 title and subtitle.
• Strong study positioning statement.
• Primary actions: View Preview and Buy Study – €249 net.
• Large study-cover visual / mock-up.
Study introduction & key themes
• Why the hotel industry is undergoing structural change.
• Eight key theses / future scenarios.
• Automation readiness system.
• Visual content blocks for major themes.

Event registration
• Directly integrated registration forms; no external event platform.
• Fields: first/last name, company, function, email address, event selection.
• Separate Berlin and Zurich registration paths/forms.
• Registrations visible in the WordPress backend.
• CSV/Excel export of participant data.
• Automatic registration confirmation email.
• Registration should support the stated limited-capacity / confirmation workflow.
Licensing & sales
• Single license: €249 net — direct purchase and Stripe payment.
• Team license: €1,490 net — request workflow for up to 10 users.
• Company license: from €2,490 net — request workflow for larger teams/hotel groups/companies.
• Secure digital delivery of the study after successful purchase.
• Statutory VAT wording as supplied by the client.
